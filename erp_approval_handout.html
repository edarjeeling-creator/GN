1 Executive Summary & Strategic Rationale

As Gyanoday Niketan continues its expansion in academic scale and excellence, paper registers, disconnected spreadsheets, and fragmented records introduce administrative delays, manual errors, and communication gaps with parents. The Gyanoday Niketan ERP is a purpose-built, cloud-based institutional operating system designed to unify school governance, streamline examination logistics, strengthen fee controls, and establish structured support for ICSE and NEP 2020 Holistic Progress Card (HPC) requirements.

Core Mandate: Consolidate institutional governance into one single source of truth—providing leadership with real-time academic and financial visibility, relieving teachers of routine tabulation, and delivering parents a transparent window into their child's progress.
2 Leadership Impact Matrix: The 4 Administrative Heads

The platform addresses the exact governance responsibilities of our four administrative leaders.

The Director Governance & Finance
  • Executive Financial Visibility: Real-time collection metrics, outstanding fee categorization, and automated digital ledger reconciliation.
  • Institutional Data Sovereignty: Complete school ownership on dedicated encrypted cloud storage—eliminating third-party lock-in.
  • Institutional Distinction: Positions Gyanoday Niketan as a technology-first leader, enhancing admissions appeal and parent trust.
The Principal Academic Leadership & 360° HPC
  • Executive Academic Moderation & Approval: Final institutional moderation authority across all terms; digital sign-off and publication controls.
  • NEP 2020 360° Holistic Progress Card (HPC): Full oversight of all 4 developmental voices (Teacher, Student self-reflection, Peer review, Parent home observation).
  • Quality Assurance & Standards: Cross-class performance benchmarks, rubric adherence, and early remedial learning intervention.
The Headmaster Senior Academics & Discipline
  • Senior School Academic Oversight: Instant analysis of Class 9–10 ICSE performance, subject-wise bottlenecks, and board readiness.
  • One-Click Examination Review: Rapid verification and moderation of terminal mark sheets, grading curves, and student ranks.
  • Punctuality & Discipline Tracking: Real-time monitoring of chronic student absenteeism and faculty attendance patterns.
The Chief Co-ordinator Operational Rhythm & Faculty Engine
  • Conflict-Free Routine Engine: Automated timetable generation with instant teacher substitution management during leave.
  • Weekly Assessment Cadence: Standardized test scheduling across all sections without syllabus collision or classroom logjams.
  • Departmental Synchronization: Seamless coordination between classroom teachers, administrative offices, and library facilities.

4 Enterprise Data Security & Institutional Governance

The platform enforces structured security and governance controls to protect student welfare, academic records, and institutional financial data.

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
Teachers only access their designated classes; accountants cannot modify academic marks; only Administrative Heads possess institutional oversight and final card publication authority.
Database Row-Level Security (RLS)
Database policies support tenant and student isolation. Direct unauthorized API requests are rejected at the database level.
Immutable Audit Logging
Significant actions—including mark modifications, fee receipts, and HPC publications—are logged with timestamp and user ID for accountability.
Encrypted Cloud Backups
Nightly automated backups are designed to reduce institutional data-loss risk due to hardware failures or local device damage.
